UAMSWP Shortcodes UI adds ready-made content elements (alerts, callouts, buttons, column rows, embeds, the UAMS calendar and map) to WordPress posts and pages. Editors insert them from the classic editor through the [Shortcake (Shortcode UI)](https://wordpress.org/plugins/shortcode-ui/) plugin, which gives every shortcode a form instead of hand-typed attributes.

This wiki holds the documentation: a walkthrough, task guides, the shortcode and hook reference, and troubleshooting. The [README](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/UAMSWP-shortcodes-ui#readme) keeps an overview, the requirements, installation and development notes.
